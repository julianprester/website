Julian Prester

I am a Senior Lecturer of Business Information Systems at The University of Sydney in Australia. I received my PhD as a Scientia Scholar in Information Systems from the UNSW Business School. Originally from Germany, I graduated with a Master of Science (with honors) in Management Information Systems and Bachelor of Science in Business Informatics from the University of Regensburg.

I study all-remote organising, how organising works when there is no shared place. I am a qualitative field researcher and I study born-digital organisations ethnographically, with process-relational theory as my signature. Digital nomadism is the individual extreme of the same story.

In my spare time I enjoy reading books and writing code. I love travelling the world and believe a passport full of stamps is way better than an apartment full of stuff.
